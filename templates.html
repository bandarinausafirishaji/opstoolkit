Container Inspection Checklist

A practical checklist for recording the condition and key inspection points of an empty shipping container before receiving, releasing or transferring it.

  • Container Number
  • ISO Size / Type
  • External Condition
  • Doors and Locking Mechanism
  • Floor Condition
  • Roof Condition
  • Side Walls and End Wall
  • CSC Plate / Identification
  • Cleanliness
  • Damage Remarks
  • Inspection Date
  • Inspector Name and Signature
