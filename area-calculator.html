How to Calculate Square Metres

For a rectangular area, multiply the length by the width.

Area = Length × Width

Example: a warehouse measuring 20 metres by 15 metres has an area of 300 m².

Where Can This Calculator Be Used?

This calculator can be useful for estimating warehouse floor area, container yard space, cargo storage areas, work areas, parking spaces and other operational planning requirements.

Important Note

Results are mathematical estimates based on the measurements entered. For operational, engineering or construction decisions, verify dimensions and site conditions independently.
