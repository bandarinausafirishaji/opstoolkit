What Is Volumetric Weight?

Volumetric weight, also called dimensional weight, estimates the space occupied by cargo rather than relying only on its physical weight.

The basic formula used by this calculator is:

Volumetric Weight = Length × Width × Height × Number of Pieces ÷ Divisor

Chargeable Weight

For the calculation shown here, chargeable weight is the higher of total actual weight and total volumetric weight.

The applicable divisor and billing rules can vary by carrier, transport mode and service. Always confirm the applicable rate with the carrier or freight provider before using the result for commercial billing.

Example

If one package measures 60 cm × 40 cm × 50 cm, its volume is 0.12 cubic metres. With a divisor of 5000, the calculated volumetric weight is 24 kg.

Operational Use

This calculator can support preliminary freight planning, shipment quoting, cargo comparison and transport planning where dimensional weight is applicable.
