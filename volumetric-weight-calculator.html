Calculation Basis

What Is Volumetric Weight?

Volumetric weight, also known as dimensional weight, estimates the weight equivalent of the space occupied by cargo. It is useful when cargo is relatively bulky compared with its actual physical weight.

What Is Chargeable Weight?

In the calculation used here, the estimated chargeable weight is the higher of the actual weight and the calculated volumetric or equivalent weight.

Why Does the Factor Change?

The conversion factor is not universal. It can vary according to transport mode, carrier, service and tariff. Therefore, this calculator provides reference methods rather than replacing the carrier's official rating rules.

Transport Modes

Air Freight: Dimensional weight is commonly calculated using a divisor. This tool uses 6000 as a general reference when dimensions are entered in centimetres.

Ocean LCL: LCL rating commonly uses a weight-versus-measurement basis. This calculator uses 1 cubic metre as a reference equivalent to 1,000 kg for preliminary comparison.

Rail Freight: This tool uses 3000 as a reference divisor for preliminary calculations.

Road Freight: The applicable conversion factor can vary by carrier, country, service and tariff. Use the custom factor where required.

Custom: Enter the divisor or conversion factor provided by your carrier or freight forwarder.

Important Note

This calculator is intended for preliminary planning and estimation. Always confirm the applicable chargeable-weight rule, divisor, density ratio and tariff with the relevant carrier or freight provider before using the result for commercial billing.
