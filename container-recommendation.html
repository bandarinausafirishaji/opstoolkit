Reference Capacities

The following approximate internal volume capacities are used for planning:

20ft Dry Container: approximately 33 CBM

40ft Dry Container: approximately 67 CBM

40ft High Cube: approximately 76 CBM
Planning Note:
Container selection should not be based on CBM alone. Cargo dimensions, weight, loading arrangement, axle or payload limitations, cargo type and actual container internal dimensions must also be considered.
